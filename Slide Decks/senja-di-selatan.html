The Southern Indonesia Sunset Ring Collection

Senja di Selatan

Twilight in the South

At the precise moment the sun descends beyond the Indian Ocean horizon off the southern coast of the Indonesian archipelago, an extraordinary convergence of light occurs — amber and sienna rays fracture across the deep, rhythmic swells, casting molten gold across an infinite expanse of water. This collection translates that ephemeral natural phenomenon into wearable high jewellery: the warmth of a single luminous stone embodying the sun itself, cradled within a shank that carries the memory of the ocean's movement.

03 — Design Architecture

Anatomical Structure
& Sketch Visualization

Centre Stone Setting
Six-claw double-prong configuration in polished 18K Rose Gold, engineered to maximise the entry of ambient and incident light into the pavilion facets. Each prong tapers to a fine point, visually evoking the last rays of the sun breaking the horizon.
The Shank — Bypass Architecture
An intertwining bypass shank in satin-finished 18K Rose Gold, featuring engineered wave-like undulations in the shank profile. The two bands converge asymmetrically beneath the centre stone, referencing the convergence of ocean currents.
Pavé Shoulder Accents
Micro-pavé set round brilliant-cut diamonds (E–F / VS1–VS2) bead-set along the ascending shoulders, their collective scintillation replicating the shimmering reflection of golden light across a moving water surface.

05 — Pre-Production Pipeline

Craftsmanship
Roadmap

Before a single gram of gold is poured, the Senja di Selatan commission passes through four exacting pre-production phases — each a discipline unto itself, and collectively the foundation upon which all tangible craft rests.

Phase I
Gemstone Sourcing & Calibration
Global procurement of the focal Fancy Vivid Yellow-Orange radiant diamond, subject to independent GIA grading verification and light performance analysis (ASET / Ideal-Scope). Concurrent curation of the pavé melee parcel: each stone calibrated for colour and brilliance matching to ensure chromatic coherence across the pavé field.
Phase II
3D CAD Engineering — Rhinoceros / Matrix
Conversion of 2D concept sketches into precision three-dimensional parametric models. This phase governs: exact gallery geometry and prong clearances, the bypass shank curvature ratio (wave undulation amplitude and frequency), pavé channel depth, and structural finite-element analysis to ensure the intertwining shank retains torsional integrity under daily wear.
Phase III
Rapid Prototyping — High-Fidelity Resin Wax Print
Production of a 1:1 scale wax resin model via SLA/DLP photopolymer printing at 25–50 micron layer resolution. Client fitting session: assessment of ring size, bypass proportion on the finger, visual weight balance, and stone seat verification. Prong tip geometry and pavé seat depth are inspected under 10× loupe before approval to proceed.
Phase IV
Casting Preparation — Lost-Wax Investment Method
Following client sign-off on the wax prototype: spruing the model within a casting tree, encasing in high-strength investment plaster (colloidal silica compound), de-waxing in a programmable kiln at 750°C to eliminate the resin, and final cavity inspection prior to centrifugal casting of the 18K rose gold alloy. The lost-wax methodology ensures micron-level fidelity between prototype and cast form.
"Each phase is not a step toward the jewel — it is the jewel, forming."
